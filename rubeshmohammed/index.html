01 / The person behind the journey

Experience built over time.
Perspective built through people.

From airline reservations to regional leadership, and from commercial operations to entrepreneurship — a career shaped by movement, responsibility and relationships.

“A meaningful journey is measured not only by where it takes you, but by the people you meet and the values you carry along the way.”

I’m Mohammed Rubesh — an aviation professional and entrepreneur with over 20 years of international experience across full-service, low-cost and hybrid airline environments.

My professional journey began in airline reservations and grew through sales, station operations, commercial leadership and market development. I have worked with Qatar Airways, Oman Air, Bahrain Air and flydubai, taking on responsibilities across South Asia and the GCC.

Along the way, I have helped launch airline stations, build travel-trade relationships, lead teams, strengthen regional sales networks and improve commercial performance. Those experiences continue to inform how I approach business today.

Today, I serve as Founder & MD of Ekal Ventures, bringing an international outlook and an entrepreneurial mindset to new opportunities.
